### Approved aesthetic, not literal graphic/production master

- Premium department-store presentation, warm forest-green/cream/white product palette, reserved and tasteful placement.
- Preserve the previously approved hat scale and the approved quarter-zip dark-green-on-green / cream-on-green / green-on-cream treatments.
- Approved logo usage: **no gold** on ordinary merchandise. Reserve gold seal for formal/selected special editions.
- All V7 brand art is immutable. The concepts were image-generated and any rendered horse or seal must be **replaced**, never assumed to match exact master path geometry.
- Correct horse is the stylized **horse head and mane**, NEVER a standing or galloping four-legged horse. Correct rope seal is the original owner-approved locked rope version.
- Wordmark/type must come from approved outlined SVG masters; never retype from approximate screenshot text.

## Store image requirements

Each approved sellable product variant needs independent product-only front (white background), product-only back/reverse (if decorated), and one diverse, age-appropriate model view for applicable clothing (Men/Women/Girls/Boys). Add imprint close-up when beneficial. Target 3:4 2400×3200 master, optimize for mobile and desktop; do **not** use contact sheets as per-product product-listing media.

Use Printful blank/mockup photography as a geometry/color reference. **Create blank garment photography first**, composite exact approved SVGs onto the garments in a deterministic image editing step, inspect at print and 100% image resolution, and confirm print areas and samples. Nonexistent Printful colorways or models must not be advertised as purchasable.
