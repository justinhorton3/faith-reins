## Design rules protected by this lock

1. **Do not regenerate, repaint, or overwrite these six selected concept images.** Preserve as the approved baseline even after Printful options are chosen.
2. Hat: forest green, understated cream horse-head embroidery, centered on front crown. The hat logo is the reduced-scale version previously selected.
3. Quarter-zip: forest green, small cream horse-head left-chest artwork; clean premium retail fit.
4. Vest: tailored-looking forest green diamond-quilted silhouette, small cream horse-head left-chest artwork.
5. Clean seamless white product photography with paired model shots, realistic fabric and hardware.
6. No gold applied to the logo in ordinary Faith Reins merchandise. Gold-toned *garment zippers* shown in the selected vest concept are product hardware, not a change to the brand artwork.
7. **Do not substitute a standing/four-legged horse** for the approved V7 horse-head SVG. Final production print/embroidery designs must use the exact `approved/horse/horse-master-green.svg`, properly prepared for each ink/thread color.
8. Do not silently replace premium quilted vest with a generic fleece vest to match Printful catalog. Mark that as a sourcing gap requiring owner choice.
