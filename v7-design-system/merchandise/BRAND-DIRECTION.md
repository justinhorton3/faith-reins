# Faith Reins Merchandise Brand Direction

**Status:** Direction approved for concept development; product selection, pricing, sourcing, and store launch remain separate decisions.  
**Applies to:** V7 merchandise, product photography, catalog presentation, and future shop experience.

## 1. Brand idea

Faith Reins should feel like a **premium lifestyle label with a meaningful mission**, not a nonprofit gift shop.

The merchandise should be desirable on its own merits. The mission adds meaning and connection, but products must still feel considered, well-designed, and worth buying if someone encounters the brand before learning the story.

The reference point is the experience and quality cues of a high-end department store: elevated everyday goods, confident styling, strong materials, thoughtful product photography, and a coherent collection.

## 2. Principles

- **Premium before promotional.** Avoid giveaway-style swag, cluttered layouts, oversized logo use on every item, and generic fundraising aesthetics.
- **A collection, not a pile of products.** Apparel, accessories, and home goods should share a recognizable visual point of view.
- **Wearable and usable.** Product quality, fit, materials, and everyday usefulness matter as much as branding.
- **Color is intentional.** Offer thoughtful colorways where product and supplier options allow; do not limit the collection to one branded color.
- **Show real-life context.** Use people/models across relevant age groups and styles so customers can understand fit, scale, and how products look in use.
- **Branding has range.** Some products can feature the full Faith Reins identity prominently; others should use a restrained mark, seal, or small placement.
- **Mission remains present, not pushy.** Explain how shopping connects to Faith Reins without making every product page feel like a donation appeal.

## 3. Product universe

Treat the following as an exploration space, not a commitment to stock every category.

### Apparel
- Adult unisex, women's, and youth tees
- Polos, hoodies, and hats
- Elevated outerwear, especially a men's quilted or utility-style vest; explore a women's vest if the fit and supplier quality support it
- Additional premium casual layers as the collection develops

### Everyday accessories
- Keychains, stickers, notebooks, and pens
- Mugs and insulated thermoses / water bottles

### Home and gifting
- Throw blankets
- Thoughtful giftable accessories and coordinated sets

Start with a focused, achievable assortment and expand after product quality, supplier availability, margins, and customer response are validated.

## 4. Visual direction

- Aim for refined outdoor / heritage lifestyle retail, warm and approachable rather than flashy or overly corporate.
- Use calm, natural settings and clean studio product photography where appropriate.
- Mix editorial lifestyle images with clear product-detail views.
- Show garments on models as well as product-only views; vary adult and youth models where relevant to the item.
- Make color options easy to compare without turning the page into a noisy grid.
- Keep styling, lighting, cropping, and backgrounds consistent enough that the catalog reads as one brand.
- Prioritize visible fabric, construction, finish, fit, scale, and practical use.

## 5. Logo and asset integrity

Use only the approved V7 assets registered in the repository, including the approved horse icon, website lockups, and seal.

- Do not ask image-generation tools to redraw, approximate, or invent the Faith Reins logo or horse icon.
- Generate or source neutral product/lifestyle imagery first, then apply the exact approved SVG artwork as a separate compositing step.
- Preserve approved proportions, spacing, colors, and wording. Do not stretch, trace, alter, or improvise logo variants.
- Select logo treatment by product and application; not every item needs a large mark.
- Follow the V7 asset register and merchandise image-system specification for exact asset selection and quality checks.

## 6. Shop experience guardrails

- Merchandise supports the mission; it does not replace or obscure charitable giving.
- Keep Donate as the primary action and Shop as a normal desktop navigation item.
- On mobile, keep Shop within the hamburger menu, away from the primary donation action.
- Do not add site search.
- Present products as a curated collection with clear categories, color/size variants, and straightforward product details.
- Do not launch or change the live site as a consequence of documenting this direction alone.

## 7. Decisions still to make

Before production, validate:
- Suppliers, garment blanks, materials, decoration methods, sizing, and color availability
- Unit economics, pricing, shipping, returns, and inventory approach
- Which products belong in the first release versus a later collection
- Final logo placement and scale per product
- Product photography and image deliverables for each SKU
- Whether premium outerwear (including the men's vest) can meet quality and margin expectations

## 8. Recommended next step

Create a visual concept board for the collection before building product listings. Include a small but representative cross-section: men's vest on a model, women's and youth apparel, a polo or hoodie, a hat, a water bottle/thermos, a throw blanket, and a notebook/pen or keychain. Show a few thoughtful colorways. Use the board to align on the overall feel, then shortlist real products and suppliers.

This document records brand direction only. It does not approve a supplier, final assortment, price, store build, or live-site change.


## Approved merchandise logo treatment — 2026-10-12

**Owner-approved decision: Standard merchandise is one-color; NO gold in the core collection.** Use the existing locked one-color V7 assets, never AI-generated approximations.

- **Light substrates:** approved solid forest-green transparent seal (shirts, cream hoodies, mugs, notebooks, thermoses, packaging as appropriate).
- **Dark substrates:** approved solid white transparent seal (forest-green vests, polos, hats and dark apparel). Cream-colored physical thread/ink is an optional production match only after sampling; do not relabel or recolor the locked SVG.
- **Premium understated placement:** use the approved horse icon alone, particularly small left-chest embroidery on vests/polos and embroidery on caps. Placement and viable minimum size remain subject to vendor proof.
- **Full seal:** use selectively on graphic tees, hoodies and gift goods where size supports legible lettering and rope.
- **Gold:** reserve the approved two-color seal for formal communications, certificates, donor gifts or deliberately designated limited/special editions. It is *not* the default everyday merchandise decoration.

**This is an approved branding rule, not authorization to launch SKUs, order production, or publish the shop.** All supplier sew-outs, print proofs, and pricing remain pending.
