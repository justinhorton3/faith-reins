## Why Printful

Wix and Printful support a native on-demand integration: customer purchase -> payment from merchant to Printful -> production -> Printful ships to buyer. Inventory need not be pre-purchased.

Vendor documentation:
- https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50262542898193-How-do-I-connect-Wix-with-Printful
- https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50262554999441-How-do-I-configure-and-sync-my-Wix-products
- https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-dropshipping-with-printful
- https://www.printful.com/custom-embroidery

**Account preparation:** Wix Stores must be active; appropriate Wix commerce plan needed to process payments. Merchant must connect a Printful account and payment method and configure customer-facing shipping, taxes and returns. These actions require account-owner authorization. Do not publish products without separate approval.

### Design color rules
- On white or cream garments, use dark/forest-green one-color artwork.
- On forest-green garments, use cream/white artwork.
- Approved quarter-zip variants also include subtle tonal dark green on green.
- **No gold on standard merchandise.** Formal and explicitly approved special editions can use the gold seal.

## Vendor-selection gate — before generating final retail photography

For each ID, record:
1. Exact Printful product name, catalog identifier and manufacturer/blank SKU.
2. Fabric composition, fabric weight, cut/fit and available colors matched to Faith Reins green/cream/white.
3. Men/women/girls/boys availability and size guides — do not fabricate youth versions of adult garments.
4. Decoration options and exact print/embroidery field bounds for front/back.
5. Per-variant supplier base price, print/embroidery fee, shipping fees and expected retail gross margin.
6. Current sample availability and fulfillment/return policy.
7. Link to live Printful catalog product and exported unbranded product photographs/mockups.
8. Review of whether this product honestly reproduces the already-approved premium concept.

If Printful doesn't offer an acceptable match for QZIP-001 or VEST-001, mark **CATALOG GAP**. Do NOT replace or downgrade the locked concept without the owner's approval. Investigate alternatives only after reporting the gap.

## Three-image minimum per approved size/color/fit variant

1. **Product-only front** on a neutral seamless-white background.
2. **Product-only back** displaying exactly the approved back design (or plain back for embroidered polos).
3. **Modeled product photo**, showing fit and age-appropriate diversity; alternate angle/detail is an optional fourth.

Use supplier original product photography as the primary garment reference. Generated blank-product/model photography is concept imagery only; apply exact approved logos through deterministic compositing and inspect each result, not an image model hallucinating a logo. Do not render contact sheets as stand-ins for individual storefront image files.

Preferred canonical masters: 2400×3200 3:4 with responsive WebP derivatives; preserve source photos and separate final logo overlays. Respect Printful asset licensing and product depiction terms.

## Suggested operational sequence

1. Compare live Printful catalog blanks against HAT-001, QZIP-001 and VEST-001 first, without replacing approved design references.
2. Upload exact source vectors/compatible high-res print assets to Printful; confirm embroidery digitization where required. Do not send altered or AI-invented logos.
3. Build unpublished product drafts or templates; create a supplier proof using Printful design maker.
4. Order physical samples for a hat, quarter-zip, vest if available, and one tee; verify color, garment feel, placement, embroidery and DTG performance.
5. Approve product and photo quality, then sync to Wix as **draft/unpublished** listings for review.
6. Publish only after explicit owner approval, including margin, shipping and policy review.
