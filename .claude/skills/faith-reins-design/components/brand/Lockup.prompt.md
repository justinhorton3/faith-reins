The primary Faith Reins logo — use in headers, footers, title slides and documents.

```jsx
<Lockup size="sm" assetBase="assets/logo/" />
<Lockup tone="dark" markColor="gold" />
```

- `tone`: light (cream ground) · dark (green ground)
- `size`: sm 44px horse · md 84px · lg 120px
- Keep 10% of logo height as clear space; never re-typeset the wordmark elsewhere.
