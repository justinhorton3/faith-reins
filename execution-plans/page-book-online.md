## Copy

Hero headline: Request an Appointment  
Hero body: Tell us a little about your needs and our team will follow up with next steps.  
Primary CTA: Submit request

## Layout Instructions

- Build as an inquiry/request page.
- Include concise form fields: parent/guardian name, phone, email, child age, service interest, message.
- Add privacy note: Do not include urgent or emergency information in this form.

## Validation

- Desktop: form labels are clear.
- Tablet: form fields remain full-width or balanced.
- Mobile: inputs are easy to tap and submit button is visible.
