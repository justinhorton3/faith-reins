Description & details

Designed to move from a morning at the barn to the rest of your day, with a simple silhouette and restrained Faith Reins identity.

  • Proposed white pique body with forest-green horse embroidery
  • Short sleeves, a flat-knit collar and cuffs
  • Side vents and matching button rims on the proposed blank

The images show the approved visual concept. Final production photos will follow the decorated sample, including the smaller approved horse scale.

Fabric & care

White fit reference: 65% polyester / 35% cotton pique, 5.0 oz/yd².

For the proposed embroidery: cold gentle wash inside out with mild detergent; cool-air tumble dry. Iron from the reverse between cloths without steam on the embroidery. Final care follows the approved garment label.

Fit & measurements

The proposed White blank has a relaxed cut that Printful describes as running large. Measure a polo you already love and compare flat width and length.

Styled images are visual references. The model’s measurements and worn size have not been verified.

Delivery & returns

Delivery dates and shipping charges will appear when ordering is available. Made-to-order production and shipping are separate steps; no delivery date is promised in this preview.

Garment measurements in inches

Measure a laid-flat polo: length from high point shoulder to hem; width across the chest; sleeve from center-back collar over the shoulder to sleeve hem.

Garment measurements may vary by up to 2 in / 5 cm. Body chest is a separate circumference reference. Centimeters are converted at 2.54 cm/in. This chart applies only to the proposed White blank.
