## Findings that change the plan

- Youth polos: no exact child-specific polo blank located; no adult measurements assigned to children.
- Quarter-zips: ST357 and Comfort Colors 1580 list DTFlex; reviewed pages do not establish the required embroidery option.
- Quilted vests: fleece/knit alternatives change the design; exact quilted blank remains unresolved.
- Cream / green polos and youth hoodies: reviewed ranges do not establish approved colors. Stone, Sand, White and Spruce are not automatically interchangeable with the brand palette.
- Mugs and bottles: printed background color is not a colored handle/interior or matte finish. A plain glossy comparison is not an exact match.
- Journals: Hardcover Journal Matte matches 5.75 × 8 inches / 150 pages. JournalBook is a different size/page count. Include the back barcode in production proofs.
- Throws: BP40 supports the size and corner embroidery, but its reviewed face colors do not establish cream/forest green.

## Sizing evidence and limitations

Numerical charts are stored in each applicable product MD and in the structured audit. K500 separates body circumference from flat garment width; tolerance up to 2 inches / 5 cm is retained. Centimeters are explicitly converted at 2.54 cm per inch, rather than presented as separately observed metric values. The women’s chart retains raw A/B/C labels because its diagram descriptions did not render; the youth hoodie’s sleeve method conflicts with its values and needs supplier clarification. Neither should be published as a complete shopper size guide until resolved. Blocked concepts retain comparison data only; they have no approved sellable size set.

## Common launch gates

1. Select exact blank and approved palette/fit/decoration without silently redesigning the concept.
2. Configure product and color-size-location variant IDs; check inventory and fulfillable region.
3. Approve artwork dimensions, digitization, decorated sample, final care and accurate product photography.
4. Set retail prices, margin, taxes, shipping calculation and checkout mapping.
5. Approve Faith Reins shipping, returns and support policy; supplier terms alone are not the customer-facing policy.
6. Complete desktop/mobile visual and interaction review before a deliberate website handoff with independent website asset copies.
