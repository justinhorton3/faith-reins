## Care, delivery and returns

- **Candidate care:** Cold gentle wash inside out with mild detergent; cool-air tumble drying; iron on reverse between cloths without steaming the embroidery. Final garment label and decorated sample must be checked. [Supplier care reference](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50263252811537-How-do-I-take-care-of-my-products).
- **Delivery:** Supplier describes typical fulfillment of 2–5 business days, with shipping additional and dependent on destination/availability. This is an estimate, not a Faith Reins delivery promise. Requote the exact configured variant at checkout. [Source](https://www.printful.com/shipping).
- **Returns:** Supplier normally excludes change-of-mind and wrong-size/color returns; supplier defect/loss claims have a 30-day reporting window. Faith Reins customer policy, applicable exceptions and contact workflow must be finalized before launch. [Supplier policy](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50263870468753-What-is-Printful-s-return-and-refund-policy).
- **Ordering:** Not enabled. Price, taxes, shipping, supplier variant mapping and customer policy must be approved before a live add-to-bag action.
