## Layout and handoff constraints

- Review at representative 1440px, 1024px, 768px, 767px, 390px and 320px widths. The existing website switches hero sources at 767px and uses fixed hero heights; these portrait sources need a reviewed composition rather than an automatic wide crop.
- Reserve space for the headline and CTA. The current hero uses light text and a dark scrim; a white/ivory product composition will need a deliberate text/contrast treatment scoped to Shop. Do not change all page heroes as a side effect.
- Use contain layouts or reviewed focal crops that retain the full horse embroidery and garment shape. Avoid cropping across faces or chest marks. Preserve source aspect ratio and intrinsic dimensions.
- Keep the full Faith Reins identity, restrained forest/ivory palette and existing navigation. Do not overlay unsupported product, pricing or supplier claims.
- Choose descriptive alt text if the final composition conveys collection information beyond adjacent copy; keep purely decorative treatment consistent with the actual page meaning.
- After selection, commit independent copies/derivatives under the website's own public asset paths. Record version, source hash and derivative settings as informational provenance. Never import from brand during build or runtime.
