Approved October 17, 2026. This document describes navigation ownership, not a claim that every future account capability is already live.

Home is reached through the logo. Donate is a separate header action to `/give`. Utility: phone, Contact, My Account, Bag. Remove duplicate provider utility link. A member account entry is proposed at `/account`; actual supported capabilities must be verified before connecting login or self-service.

## Ownership

- About owns mission, people, qualifications, partners and the herd.
- Therapies owns service descriptions. EAL is experiential learning, distinguished from clinical therapy.
- Families owns preparation, callback access, payment and insurance.
- Providers owns clinical fit, referral requirements and coordination.
- Get Involved owns donor/volunteer journeys, stewardship, giving and sponsorships. Horse sponsorship is one section of the shared sponsorship page.
- One FAQ page groups questions by audience and subject.
- Existing Join Our Team URL remains the careers destination; retain old volunteer section links and point them to the dedicated volunteer journey.
- Footer links the full sitemap and existing policies. Existing live routes remain valid.

## Personas

| Persona | Evaluation | Action | Returning visit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Families | Can you help my child; can we trust the team? | Request a callback | Visit guidance, contact, insurance |
| Providers | Is this a credible extension of patient care? | Connect with intake / referral | Referral guidance and coordination |
| Donors | Is the mission credible and worth continued support? | Give / sponsor | Giving history, receipts, recurring gifts where supported |
| Volunteers | Can I contribute; what is expected? | Volunteer inquiry | Orientation and volunteer contact |
| Shoppers | What can I buy; how do I manage it? | Browse / checkout | Orders, shipping and profile where supported |

## Account boundaries

One visible account entry should lead to documented supported authentication and account services. Orders, giving and profile are intended account areas, not a license to mock them. Confirm shared authentication; label separate systems if required. Keep guest routes available where supported. Volunteer accounts are deferred until scheduling needs exist. A future clinical portal remains separate.
