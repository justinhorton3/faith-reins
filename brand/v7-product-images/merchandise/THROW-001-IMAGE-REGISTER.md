### Supplier and production check

Printful lists [Embroidered Premium Sherpa Blanket | Port Authority BP40](https://www.printful.com/custom/blankets/personalized/embroidered-premium-sherpa-blanket-port-authority-bp40), 50 × 60 in, fleece face/sherpa reverse, with **Right corner** or **Large right corner** embroidery placement. These images explore that placement and fabric format, but the requested cream/forest-green blanket base colors have **not been verified as offered by Printful**. Printful's [standard embroidery thread palette](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50263985175953-How-should-I-prepare-my-design-for-embroidery) also does not list exact #173A2E forest green or #F3EEE4 cream; a nearest-match thread may differ. No Printful product is created or purchasable in these exact colors yet.

Before any listing, confirm selectable blank colors and embroidery placement in Printful's live Design Maker, use the actual approved SVG to prepare an embroidery file, review digitization for minimum strokes and negative spaces, then sample the physical color and stitching. Image generation does not prove production fidelity.
